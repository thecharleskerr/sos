A refurbished iPhone can cost hundreds of pounds less than the same model new, but only if the seller's own page states a new battery, a working warranty and a clear return window. Skip that check and you can end up with a phone that has none of the cover the word refurbished is supposed to promise, for not much less than new on a contract would have cost. This guide sets out exactly what Apple's and Samsung's own UK refurbished pages state, what gov.uk says about your rights when you buy second hand from a business, and how to weigh the total cost against a new phone on a 24 month contract. It takes about ten minutes.

## What does Apple's own refurbished programme include?

Apple's UK refurbished page states that every refurbished iOS device comes with a new battery and a new outer shell, packaged in a new white box with the accessories and cables it shipped with new. Apple backs this with a standard one year limited warranty on every Apple Certified Refurbished product, and you can add AppleCare on top for longer cover. Apple's UK returns page states you have the right to cancel and return within 14 days of taking possession, the same window as a new purchase.

Apple's refurbished page also states savings of up to 15% against the price of the same model new. Apple's UK refurbished store currently lists iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 models, so check the live listing for the model and storage you want rather than assuming a price.

## Is Samsung's Certified Re-Newed programme any different in the UK?

Yes, and the warranty runs longer. Samsung's UK Certified Re-Newed page states every device is refurbished by Samsung-approved technicians using 100% genuine Samsung parts, including a certified new battery, and comes with the same two year warranty as a new Samsung phone. Samsung's newsroom announcement of the UK launch states that eligibility for the programme in the UK, France and Germany is limited to top-grade returned products only. Samsung's UK Certified Re-Newed page states any cosmetic marks left on a unit are minor and do not affect how it works.

Neither UK page we checked states a return window or a savings percentage for Certified Re-Newed, so those stay "not stated" until Samsung publishes one.

## Here is how to check a refurbished iPhone is worth it, in about ten minutes

1. Open the model's own listing on apple.com/uk or samsung.com/uk and confirm the page states a new battery. If it does not say so, treat the battery as used and price it accordingly.
2. Note the warranty length stated on that same page. Apple's Certified Refurbished carries one year. Samsung's Certified Re-Newed carries two.
3. Check the return window stated on the seller's own page before you pay. Apple states 14 days from taking possession.

5. Compare that total to a new phone contract's total cost from [the card above](/blog/cheapest-iphone-18-deal/), including its stated price rise.
6. Take the lower total, provided the warranty and return window are ones you are willing to accept.

## Will the battery already be worn down?

Not on the two programmes we checked. Apple's UK refurbished page states every refurbished iOS device gets a new battery, not a tested used one, and Samsung's UK Certified Re-Newed page makes the same claim for its devices. That is what separates an official refurbished programme from an unbranded listing. An unbranded seller rarely states whether the battery is new, tested or simply described as good, and where a listing does not say, treat it as the original battery.

## What are my consumer rights if it breaks?

The same ones you get with a new phone, provided you bought it from a business. Gov.uk's guidance on accepting returns states that you have the same rights when you are supplied with second hand goods as when you are supplied with new, and the goods must still be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. Gov.uk's consumer rights page states these rights apply to every business that sells directly to a consumer. Gov.uk also states you can reject a faulty item and get a full refund within 30 days of taking ownership, and that you keep protection for up to six months if a repair or replacement does not fix the fault.

That sits alongside whatever warranty the seller states, not instead of it. A one year or two year manufacturer warranty and your 30 day and six month statutory rights run at the same time.

## Will it look like it has been used?

Apple's refurbished page states every unit passes full functional testing before sale. Samsung's newsroom announcement states UK devices are limited to top-grade returned stock, and Samsung's UK Certified Re-Newed page describes any remaining cosmetic marks as minor and not affecting function. Neither page sets out a numbered grading scale, so if a seller advertises grades such as excellent or good without saying what each grade means, check that seller's own page for the definition before you buy, rather than assuming what it means.
