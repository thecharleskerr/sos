Send your old phone to the first place that will take it and you likely leave value on the table, because Apple, Samsung, Google Store, O2, Vodafone, Three and EE each set the quote, the payout and the deadline differently, and none of them publishes a single price list you can look up in advance. This guide sets out only what each seller's own page states: what decides your quote, whether you get cash or credit, how long the quote lasts, and what happens if the phone turns out to be in worse shape than you said. The comparison takes about ten minutes once you have your phone's model and IMEI to hand.

## What decides your trade-in quote?

Your phone's model, storage size and condition, checked by the seller you send it to. Apple's trade-in page says you need the serial number to see an accurate value, and that the figure depends on the device, model, manufacturer and condition. Samsung's how-it-works page says you confirm the make, model and condition to get a valuation, then the discount applies at checkout. Google Store's trade-in help page describes an estimate you get by entering your device's details before it goes on to inspection. O2, Vodafone, Three and EE all start from the phone's IMEI, then ask you to grade the condition, commonly something close to excellent, some screen damage, or a few issues.

None of these pages states a figure for any specific model, so this guide does not either. Run your own model through each seller's tool and compare what comes back.

## Do you get cash or credit?

It depends who you ask, and it changes what the quote is actually worth to you. Apple's trade-in page says that if you pay in full, credit goes to your original payment method up to the purchase price, with any remainder sent as an Apple Gift Card by email, and that some monthly payment purchases get the value as an instant credit instead. Samsung's process issues Samsung Credit to your Samsung account once the device passes inspection. Google Store's help page says credit goes to your original payment method, with any amount above the price of your new Pixel given as Google Store credit.

The four networks pay more flexibly. O2's recycling guide says it pays cash into your bank account, by cheque, or as a credit on your O2 bill. Vodafone's trade-in page offers an instant saving on a new Airtime plan, account credit, or a payment towards your current Phone Plan. Three offers a cash quote or a discount applied automatically to your bill. EE says you can be paid by PayPal or straight into your bank account. Cash and bank transfer spend anywhere. A gift card, store credit or bill credit only spends with that seller, so weigh that into which quote is actually the better one.

## How long does a quote last, and what if the phone does not match?

Each seller gives you a window to send the device in, and a different quote if it turns out worse than described. The figures below are what each one's own page or terms state, with "Not stated" where a page did not give a number.

| Seller | Quote valid for | Send your device within | If the condition does not match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Trade In | 14 days after you receive your new device | 14 days of receiving your new device | Revised quote, and the same applies if Find My is not switched off |
| Samsung Trade In for Samsung Credits | 7 days | 14 days of receiving your returns packaging | New offer to accept or reject, automatically accepted after 7 days |
| Google Store trade-in | Not stated | 30 days of receiving your new device | Device is returned to you if the final value comes in under 75% of the estimate |
| O2 Recycle | Not stated | Not stated | Assessed once O2 receives and tests the device, and payment follows what they find |
| Vodafone Trade-in Tool | 7 days from the date of the quote | 14 days | Not stated |
| Three trade in | 14 days | 14 days of placing the order | Regraded, with a new quote for the condition received |
| EE trade in | 14 days | 14 days | Updated offer reflecting the actual condition |

The pattern across every seller here is the same: describe the condition honestly, because a phone that arrives in worse shape gets repriced, not rejected outright.

## Is trade-in worth it, or should you sell privately or keep the phone?

Only if the total beats the alternatives once you know how it is paid. None of the pages above states what a private sale would fetch, so there is no figure to give you here, only the sum to run yourself.

1. Get a written quote from at least two of the sellers above for the same phone, in the same stated condition.
2. Work out what each quote is actually worth to you. Cash or a bank transfer is worth its full value anywhere. A gift card or bill credit is only worth its value with that seller, so discount it if you were not planning to spend there anyway.
3. Compare that against a private sale price you are confident of, minus the time and any fees of arranging it yourself.
4. Compare it again against simply keeping the phone and moving to a SIM only plan instead of a new handset contract. Our sister site, [Save on Sims](https://saveonsims.co.uk/), lists SIM only picks that are re-checked every Monday, and our guide to [iPhone 18 contract or buying outright](/blog/iphone-18-contract-vs-buying-outright/) sets out the same kind of sum for a new phone.
5. Take whichever route nets you the most once you have accounted for how restricted the credit is. A trade-in that becomes stock for someone else's next phone is also the reason a [refurbished iPhone](/blog/should-you-buy-a-refurbished-iphone/) is worth checking before you buy new.

## Here is exactly what to do

1. Back up your phone. Apple Support's trade-in preparation page says to back up before you erase anything, so your photos, contacts and messages carry over to your next device.
2. Erase all content and settings. Apple's guide describes Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPhone, then Erase All Content and Settings. Samsung and Google Store both list a factory reset as a condition of trade-in.
3. Remove the activation lock or your Google account. Apple's terms say a revised quote follows if Find My is not removed, and Vodafone's trade-in page tells iPhone owners to turn off Find My iPhone and Android owners to remove their Google account before sending the phone.
4. Get quotes from at least two places, for example Apple, Samsung or Google Store against your own network, and note how each one pays out and how long the quote lasts using the table above.
5. Send the device within the deadline the seller quoted, using the packaging or freepost label they provide, and keep the confirmation email until the credit or payment lands.

## What if my screen is cracked?

A cracked screen lowers the quote, it does not usually rule the phone out. O2's quote tool includes a condition option for some screen damage rather than treating it as ineligible. Samsung's trade-in page says you can still get value back for damaged devices with Mobile Trade In. Three and EE both assign a condition grade rather than refusing a device outright, and price the trade-in against that grade. Get more than one quote, since each seller prices screen damage differently.

## Is my phone too old to trade in?

If it has no trade-in value, most of these sellers will still take it for free rather than leave you with nothing. Apple's trade-in page says it will recycle any device for free, no matter the model or condition, if it is not eligible for a trade-in value. O2's recycling guide says a phone that will not power on is unlikely to be accepted for O2's paid recycling service, but you can still recycle it for free through the same process. Either way, an old handset does not have to go in a drawer or the bin.

## Is it safe to send my data with the phone?

It is, once you have done your own part of the job. Back up the phone, then erase all content and settings, then sign out of Find My or remove your Google account, using the steps above, before you send anything. On the seller's side, Samsung's trade-in FAQ says personal data is completely erased once your device arrives, and EE's trade-in page says it wipes every device securely as soon as it is received. Google Store's trade-in terms list a factory reset and activation lock removal as a condition of eligibility in the first place, which is one more reason to do that step yourself rather than leaving it to whoever inspects the phone.

Get a quote from at least two sellers for your exact model and condition, check how each one pays out using the table above, then run the sum against selling privately or keeping the phone on a SIM only plan.
