Miss the day your minimum term ends and you keep paying a contract price for a phone you already own outright, for as long as you do not notice.

Most phone bills fall once you move to a SIM only plan on the same host network your phone already uses, once the phone itself is paid off. Getting there means running six checks: the phone you have paid off, the data you actually use, the price rise you are carrying, any roaming add-on you do not need, the switching route, and any perk you pay for but never touch. None of them takes more than a couple of minutes, and none needs more than your last few bills and the network's own account app.

Every figure below comes from a network's own pages or from Ofcom, dated. This week's picks above are re-checked every Monday, so the price on the card is current, not something you have to go and verify yourself.

## Here is exactly what to do

1. **Check whether your minimum term has ended.** O2 splits its bill into a Device Plan, the cost of the phone, and an Airtime Plan, the cost of data, minutes and texts. EE's Flex Pay does the same, separating the handset from the airtime plan. Once the device side is paid off, only the airtime plan is left, and that is the part a SIM only deal replaces. Ofcom requires your provider to send you an end-of-contract notification between 10 and 40 days before your minimum term ends, by text, email or letter, stating the price you are on and what changes. Search your messages for it if you are not sure where you stand.
2. **Check the data you use against the data you pay for.** Open your network's app and look at the last two or three months, not one unusual one. Compare that figure with the allowance on the card above rather than the one you originally signed up to. [Our guide to unlimited data](/blog/is-unlimited-data-worth-it/) sets out when paying for unlimited is worth it and when it is not.
3. **Check the mid-contract price rise you are carrying.** O2 and Vodafone both state a rise of £2.50 a month each April. Three's rise runs from £1.80 to £2.30 a month each 1 April, depending on plan size. EE states £2.50 a month each 31 March. giffgaff, SMARTY, iD Mobile on SIM only, Lebara and Talkmobile all state no price rise. The table below sets out which of those no-rise networks shares a host with which of the big four.
4. **Check any roaming add-on you do not need.** Before you buy a pass for a trip, check your own plan's roaming page first. O2 includes up to 25GB of EU data at no extra cost. Vodafone charges £2.75 a day, and EE charges £2.59 a day unless you are on its Essentials Plus, All Rounder or Full Works plans. [Our roaming guide](/blog/best-sim-only-deal-for-eu-roaming/) has the full network by network table.
5. **Check the switching route before you commit to anything.** Ofcom's own process runs by text. Send PAC to 65075 to keep your number, or STAC to 75075 if you do not mind a new one. Ofcom's switching page states your current provider must reply with the code within a minute, that it stays valid for 30 days, and that your number should be ported and ready with your new provider within one working day of you handing the code over.
6. **Check any perk you pay for but never use.** O2's Priority is a free service open to any O2 mobile customer, according to O2's own page, so there is nothing to cut there. EE is different: its Inclusive Extras, a subscription such as Netflix or Apple Music swapped every 30 days, comes only on the pricier All Rounder and Full Works plans. If you never swap or use it, a plainer SIM only plan without it is worth pricing up. Three's Three+ and Vodafone's VeryMe Rewards are open to any customer on those networks too, and neither page states a joining cost. [Our perks guide](/blog/which-uk-network-has-the-best-perks/) lists what each network actually gives you.

## Will I lose my number if I switch network?

No, not if you get a code first. Ofcom's text to switch process exists for exactly this: send PAC to 65075 from the phone whose number you want to keep, and your current provider has to reply with the code within a minute. It stays valid for 30 days, and your number should be ported and ready with your new provider within one working day of you handing the code over. [Our switching guide](/blog/how-to-switch-mobile-network-and-keep-your-number/) walks through every step in order.

## Will moving to a cheaper brand change my signal?

Not if you stay on the same host. Only four networks in the UK own masts: EE, O2, Three and Vodafone. Every other brand you see advertised rents space on one of those four. giffgaff runs on O2's masts. SMARTY and iD Mobile run on Three's. Lebara and Talkmobile run on Vodafone's. So a switch from O2 to giffgaff, or from Three to SMARTY, gets you the same coverage at your postcode with a different bill, not a different signal. [Our coverage guide](/blog/best-uk-network-for-data/) and the [networks page](/networks/) set out the full list of who runs on whom.

## What if I am still mid-contract or the phone is not paid off?

You can still switch the SIM plan, but check what leaving early actually costs first. Leaving an airtime plan before the minimum term ends can carry an early termination charge, so ask your current provider what that figure is before you request a switching code, and weigh it against what the cheaper plan saves over the months you have left. A phone still on its own device plan is usually billed separately from the airtime, and O2's own leaving page says that ending your airtime plan within the first 24 months of a device plan still leaves you needing to pay off that device plan in full. If the phone itself is the debt you are carrying, [Save on Smartphones' guide to getting out of a phone contract early](https://saveonsmartphones.co.uk/blog/how-to-get-out-of-a-phone-contract-early/) covers that side in more detail than we do here, since our cards are SIM only.

|---|---|---|
| O2 (direct) | £2.50 a month each April (75p on data-only and smartwatch plans); device cost frozen | giffgaff, no price rise, 18 month contracts |
| Vodafone (direct) | £2.50 a month each 1 April on airtime and data plans; Device Plans unaffected | Lebara or Talkmobile, no price rise |

If your host is EE and you also pay for roaming, check that before you switch too. 1pMobile includes EU roaming with a 14GB cap and no daily charge, and Mozillion includes it across 41 EU destinations, also with no daily charge, and both run on EE's masts.

## What to do now

None of this needs a phone call to your network. The end of contract notification, the usage figures and the price rise line are all in the account app or the bills you already have, and the switching code arrives by text. The only thing worth doing before you compare is deciding whether you want to keep your number, since that decides whether you ask for a PAC or a STAC.

Run the six checks against your last two or three bills. Most of them take a couple of minutes each, and the price rise and roaming checks are already done for you in the tables above and in our fuller guides on each.
