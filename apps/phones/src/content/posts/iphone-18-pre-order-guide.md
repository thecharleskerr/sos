## Should you get a trade-in quote before you order?

Yes, and get it before you decide anything else. Apple's trade-in terms state that a quote is valid for 14 days after you receive your new device, and the old device has to be received within that same window, checked 2 September 2026. Apple also states it will revise the quote if the phone's condition does not match what you described, if Find My iPhone is still switched on, or if the device arrives late. Paying in full gets the credit applied to your original payment method, with anything left over sent as an Apple Gift Card by email, while some monthly payment purchases get an instant credit instead, checked 2 September 2026. Apple has not published a trade-in range in pounds for the iPhone 18 Pro itself, only that a device from the iPhone X onward qualifies toward a new model, checked 9 September 2026. ## Is a contract cheaper than paying £1,299 outright?

Only if its total, not its monthly price, comes in lower. A contract's total is the monthly price multiplied by its length, plus any upfront cost, plus the network's stated mid-contract price rise if the deal runs past the month it lands. O2's airtime plans rise by £2.50 a month each April and EE's rise by £2.50 a month each 31 March, both stated on their own pages and checked in September 2026, while SMARTY and giffgaff state no price rise at all on the plans they sell now, checked 2 September 2026. EE's own deals are never sold through this site's cards, in line with EE's affiliate terms, so it is named here only for its published policy. This week's pick above already carries its network's rise inside its total contract cost, so read that figure rather than the monthly price alone. ## Will a credit check hold up a device plan order?

It can, because a device plan is a form of credit and networks say so upfront. O2 states its device plan is a loan agreement that needs a credit check, subject to status, while its separate eligibility checker is not itself a credit check and does not affect your credit rating, both checked 2 September 2026. EE's Flex Pay is described as an interest free consumer credit agreement, and credit for it is subject to a credit check, status and assessment, checked 2 September 2026. Three describes its own device plans as a form of interest free credit too, spread over 12, 24 or 36 months, checked 2 September 2026. None of this is specific to the iPhone 18 Pro. It applies to a device plan on any current phone, so ask the question or run the eligibility checker before you commit rather than after.
