Buy the wrong iPhone 18 this month and you can end up with a screen size you did not want, storage you outgrow within a year, or a contract that costs more overall than paying outright. Apple's UK store lists the iPhone 18 Pro from £1,299 for 256GB, the iPhone 18 Pro Max with a bigger 6.9 inch screen at a price we could not confirm from two readings of Apple's page, and the iPhone Duo, Apple's fold, from £1,999, all checked 9 September 2026. Pre-orders for the Pro and Pro Max open at 1:00 pm on Saturday 12 September 2026, with both on sale from Friday 18 September 2026. The Duo pre-orders separately, from 16 October 2026, so it plays no part in Saturday's window. See the [iPhone 18 Pro](/phones/iphone-18-pro/), [iPhone 18 Pro Max](/phones/iphone-18-pro-max/) or [iPhone Duo](/phones/iphone-duo/) page for this week's pick, or read on for how to choose between them.

## Pro, Pro Max or Duo: what Apple has confirmed

All three share the same A20 Pro chip, Apple's newsroom confirms, checked 9 September 2026, so processing power is not what separates them. Screen, storage and price are. The table below holds only the figures two independent readings of Apple's own UK pages agreed on. Where a reading disagreed, or only one reading saw a figure, the cell reads not stated rather than a guess.

| iPhone 18 Pro Max | Not stated | 256GB | 6.9 inch | Not stated |
| iPhone Duo | £1,999 | 256GB | 7.6 inch inner, 5.4 inch outer | £66.63 |

Checked 9 September 2026, Apple's UK shop and newsroom pages. Apple's own pages state that £66.63 a month figure for the Duo against two different finance terms in different places, so this guide states the monthly amount only, not the number of months it runs for.

Pre-order timing splits the three as much as price does. The Pro and Pro Max open for pre-order at 1:00 pm on Saturday 12 September 2026 and go on sale on Friday 18 September 2026. The Duo does not pre-order until 1:00 pm on Friday 16 October 2026, and goes on sale on Friday 23 October 2026, more than a month later, so if you want a fold on launch day you are waiting regardless of what you decide here. ## Does screen size and weight decide it?

Screen size is the one figure Apple states for all three, and weight is not stated for any of them on the pages we checked, so treat weight as unconfirmed rather than assumed. The iPhone 18 Pro has a 6.3 inch display, the iPhone 18 Pro Max a 6.9 inch display, and the iPhone Duo a 7.6 inch inner display with a 5.4 inch outer display when it is folded shut, all from Apple's own pages, checked 9 September 2026. Apple's newsroom states that the Duo's outer screen gives 90 per cent of the screen area of the iPhone 18 Pro, so a folded Duo sits close to a Pro in the hand and opens into a screen bigger than the Pro Max. If you want the largest single, flat screen, that is the Pro Max. If you want a screen that folds away smaller and then opens larger still, that is the Duo. Apple has not published a UK weight figure for any of the three on the pages this guide checked, so weigh one in a shop, or check the phone's own page once Apple lists it, before you commit to a size from a spec sheet alone.

## How much storage will you actually use?

The honest way to decide is to look at how full your current phone is right now, in its own settings, rather than a national average that says nothing about your camera roll or your downloaded apps. All three iPhone 18 models start at 256GB, checked 9 September 2026 on Apple's own shop and newsroom pages. Apple's pages list higher storage tiers for each model, but our two independent readings could not agree on a UK price for most of them, so this guide is not printing them. The one exception is the Duo's 2TB tier, confirmed at £3,199, checked the same date. We are not going to hand you a made up rule of thumb for how much storage a typical owner needs, because no verified figure covers that. If you are already close to the top of your current tier, budget for a step up rather than assuming the base 256GB will do, since Apple has not confirmed what the higher tiers cost on two of these three models.

## Is a contract cheaper than buying outright?

Only if the sums say so, and the sums need the network's price rise added in, not just the monthly figure a deal card leads with. A contract's true cost is the monthly price multiplied by its length, plus any upfront cost, plus the network's stated mid-contract price rise if the deal runs past the month it lands. Networks differ here: O2's own pricing page states that its airtime plans rise by £2.50 a month each April, with the device cost frozen, checked 2 September 2026, and EE's own terms state a £2.50 a month rise each 31 March, checked 4 September 2026. EE is never sold as an affiliate deal on this site, so it is mentioned here only for its published policy, not as a deal you can click through to. Other networks state no rise at all on the plans they sell now: SMARTY and giffgaff both confirm this on their own pages, checked 2 September 2026. This week's pick above already carries its own network's total cost, rise included, so compare that single figure against Apple's outright price, £1,299 for the Pro or £1,999 for the Duo, checked 9 September 2026, plus a SIM only plan for the same months.
