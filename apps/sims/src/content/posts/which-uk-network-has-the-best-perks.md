Pick a network for perks you will use and they pay part of your bill. Pick one for perks you never redeem and you pay the difference every month until the contract ends.

## Which UK network has the best perks?

None of them wins outright, because a perk is worth what you would have spent anyway, not what the network says it is worth. O2 Priority and Three+ are free apps with standing weekly offers you can price from the page. Tesco Mobile Clubcard Prices are the one perk that freezes your monthly price for the minimum term. EE Inclusive Extras only pay off if you already buy that subscription.

Every figure below comes from the network's own page, checked on 2 September 2026. The roaming column comes from our verified table, with its own checked dates. The deal card above is re-checked every Monday. The steps take about ten minutes.

## How do you put a pound figure on a perk?

Price a perk at the money it stops you spending, then multiply by the times you would really use it in a year. O2's Priority page prints the RRP next to the £1 price. If you buy a Greggs coffee most weeks, your saving each time is that RRP less £1. If you never go to Greggs, the saving is nil, however large the RRP looks.

Four Vue tickets for £18 saves money only if four of you go to Vue once a month. EE's Inclusive Extra is worth the subscription's price only if that subscription is already on your bank statement. Sky's Piggybank only fills if you buy more data than you use, and a smaller plan may cost less than the data you are banking. VOXI's Unlimited Social Media only matters if those apps are most of your data.

When you have a yearly figure, divide it by 12. That is the perk's monthly value to you.

## Here is exactly what to do

1. List the perks you used last year, not the ones you might use. Your bank statement shows the Greggs, cinema, Netflix and Apple spend. If it is not there, the perk is worth nil.
2. Price each perk from the network's own page: the stated RRP or subscription price, less what the perk charges, times the visits you really made. Add up the year.
3. Divide by 12. Write it next to the network's name.
4. Compare the monthly price and the total contract cost of the plan that carries the perk against the cheapest plan you would otherwise take. The card above and the [homepage](/) show both, plus the mid-contract price rise in pounds and pence. See our guide to [mid-contract price rises](/blog/mid-contract-price-rises-explained/).
5. If the perk plan costs more each month than your figure from step 3, take the cheaper plan. If not, take the perk plan. Either way, [switch with a PAC code](/blog/how-to-switch-mobile-network-and-keep-your-number/) and keep your number.

## Is a perk worth paying more every month for?

Only when the perk's monthly value to you, from step 3, is larger than the extra you pay. Three's page says its Cineworld code covers two adult 2D standard seats, excluding Cineworld Dublin and Picturehouse cinemas. If your local cinema is a Picturehouse, the perk is worth nil to you. A perk never beats a cheaper plan you would not use it on.

## Do the perks stay the same for the whole contract?

Not on most networks. Three's page says offers are subject to change and tells you to check the app for current terms, exclusions and expiry dates. EE's page says Inclusive Extras are there for the full duration of your plan, and that you can swap them every 30 days. Tesco Mobile's fixed price page is the firmest: a Clubcard Price deal freezes your basic monthly usage price for the length of the minimum contract period, and its annual April increase applies only once that period ends. For every other network, the mid-contract price rise is the figure to compare, and the card above states it in pounds and pence or says no price rise.

## Can I get the perks on a cheap SIM only plan?

Usually, yes. O2's page says Priority is for all O2 mobile customers aged 16 and over. Three's page says Three+ is for Pay Monthly and Pay As You Go customers with an active account. Vodafone's page opens VeryMe to Pay Monthly customers and to Pay As You Go customers who top up at least £10 every six weeks. Tesco Mobile's Clubcard Prices page names SIM only contracts. Sky's help page says Piggybank rollover is not available on Unlimited Data plans. EE's Inclusive Extras come with All Rounder and Full Works plans, which EE's student terms also name as SIM only plans.

Perks belong to the brand, not the masts. giffgaff runs on O2's network, but O2 lists Priority for O2 mobile customers. SMARTY runs on Three, and Three lists Three+ for Three customers. Students should read our [student SIM guide](/blog/cheapest-sim-only-deal-for-students/) first, since a student discount is a perk with a stated pound value.
